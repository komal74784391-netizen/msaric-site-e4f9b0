## Selected Works

#### **Phase-Coherent Onset Detection for Overdubbed Folk Ensembles**
Wen Ruan†, <ins>Mirela Saric</ins>†, Dario Mbeki, Fumiko Arai \
*Transactions on Sound and Music Computing (TSMC), 2025* · [Paper](https://doi.org/10.5555/tsmc.2025.0142) | [Code](https://github.com/msaric-lab/phase-onset)
- Reduced onset-picking error by 27% on 940 overdubbed recordings from three regional archives.
- Shipped a reference implementation and a six-hour annotated evaluation set.

#### **Archival Reverb Signatures as a Fingerprint for Venue Provenance**
Sunniva Lunde†, <ins>Mirela Saric</ins>†, Hipolito Navarre, Yusuf Adeyemi \
*Journal on Computing and Cultural Heritage (JOCCH), 2024* · [Paper](https://doi.org/10.5555/jocch.2024.0211) | [Dataset](https://example.org/datasets/reverb-provenance)
- Matched 86% of recordings to their recording venue using reverb-tail signatures alone.
- Released a provenance dataset covering 15 venues and 2,200 archival takes.

#### **Spectral Anchoring for Cross-Version Alignment of Wax Cylinder Transfers**
<ins>Mirela Saric</ins>†, Kenji Watanuki†, Amara Diallo, Ruth Bekele \
*Journal of Computational Musicology (JCM), 2023* · [Paper](https://doi.org/10.5555/jcm.2023.118) | [Code](https://github.com/msaric-lab/spectral-anchoring)
- Aligned 3,100 early wax-cylinder transfers across five archive versions with a 0.87 correlation score.
- Released tuning-anchor annotations and an evaluation harness for cross-archive alignment.

#### **Timbre-Aware Segmentation of Unlabeled Concert Recordings**
<ins>Mirela Saric</ins>†, Piotr Halvorsen†, Lucia Ferrante \
*Proceedings of the International Society for Music Information Retrieval (ISMIR), 2021* · [Paper](https://example.org/ismir21/timbre-seg)
- Segmented 18 hours of unreleased concert audio into instrument-level timbre regions with 0.79 boundary F1.
- Documented a lightweight annotation protocol that non-specialists can apply consistently.

#### **A Reproducible Pipeline for Analyzing Ornamentation in Solo Repertoires**
<ins>Mirela Saric</ins>, Tomas Ilves, Greta Moravec \
*Digital Humanities Quarterly, 2019* · [Paper](https://example.org/dhq/2019/ornamentation)
- Encoded 620 solo performances into a searchable ornament-event index.
- Published the pipeline and validation notebooks under an open license.
